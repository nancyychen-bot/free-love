# Free Love

*A non-profit, open-source dating app that wants to lose you as a user.*

Free Love is a deliberate answer to exploitative mainstream dating apps. ~98% of Match Group's revenue comes directly from users ($3.42B of $3.48B, FY2024 10-K), so their business depends on keeping you single and subscribed. Free Love's north-star metric is the opposite: **successful exits** — people leaving because they found their person.

Built by Justin & Nancy.

## Principles in one breath

- Optimize for exits, not engagement
- No pay-to-win, ever — billing pauses when we give you nothing
- No selling data, no surveillance
- No secret ranking — matching is open and explainable
- No manufactured scarcity, no manufactured volume — introductions only when someone clears your bar
- The whole person, not just a face
- Accountability over disposability
- Safety is a product, not a settings page
- Open source as trust

## Funding model

Mandatory flat-rate subscription after a 7-day free trial. Same price for everyone. Billing pauses automatically after 21 consecutive days with zero introductions. Published finances quarterly. Any surplus is donated to organizations working on relationship health, women's safety, and LGBTQ support.
