# Free Love

*A non-profit, open-source dating app that wants to lose you as a user.*

Free Love is a deliberate answer to exploitative mainstream dating apps. Mainstream apps make ~98% of their revenue directly from users, so their business depends on keeping you single and subscribed. Free Love's north-star metric is the opposite: **successful exits** — people leaving because they found their person.

Built by Justin & Nancy.

## Principles in one breath

- Optimize for exits, not engagement
- No pay-to-win, ever
- No selling data, no surveillance
- No secret ranking — matching is open and explainable
- Substance before faces
- Accountability over disposability
- Open source as trust
