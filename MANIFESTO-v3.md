## The problem we exist to answer

Every major dating app shares one fatal conflict of interest: **their revenue depends on you staying single.**

A matched, happy couple is a churned customer. A lonely person who keeps paying is the ideal user. So the entire industry — quietly, rationally, and at enormous human cost — optimizes for *engagement* instead of *outcomes*.

Once you see that incentive, every dark pattern stops being a mystery and starts being a *strategy*:

- **The swipe is a slot machine.** Variable-ratio reinforcement — the most addictive reward schedule known — keeps your thumb moving. It was never designed to find you a person.
- **Scarcity is manufactured.** Limited likes, rationed visibility, paywalled "see who liked you." The friction is invented so you can pay to remove it.
- **Attention is auctioned.** Boosts and super-likes sell visibility to the highest bidder, so everyone else's experience quietly degrades until they pay too.
- **You are secretly ranked.** Desirability scores sort people into invisible leagues and call it "compatibility."
- **Depth is designed out.** A photo-first interface trains you to judge a human being in 400 milliseconds.
- **Ghosting is a feature, not a bug.** Infinite alternatives mean no one is ever accountable to anyone.

We are not going to out-engineer these companies. We are going to **refuse to be them.**

Because Free Love is non-profit and open source, we can adopt the one metric the incumbents structurally cannot afford:

> **Success is someone deleting the app because they found their person.**

That single inversion frees us from every dark pattern at once. We need you *gone*, happy, and telling your friends why.

## The lines we will not cross

These are load-bearing. If a future feature violates one of these, the feature is wrong — not the principle.

1. **We optimize for exits, not engagement.** Our north-star metric is successful departures. We measure them, publish them, celebrate them. We will never measure "time in app" as a goal.

2. **No pay-to-win. Ever.** Nobody's visibility is auctioned. Nobody buys an advantage. Costs are covered by transparent, flat-rate membership — same price for everyone — never by selling advantage, attention, or anxiety. **Billing pauses automatically during droughts** — if we haven't introduced you to anyone, we don't charge you. Any surplus is donated, not retained.

3. **No selling data. No ad targeting. No surveillance.** We collect the minimum, say exactly what we keep, and never monetize who you are. This is an ethic *and* a moat — the one thing they cannot copy without dismantling their business.

4. **No secret ranking.** We will not sort people into desirability tiers. Matching is open source and explainable. If we surface two people to each other, we can tell them why.

5. **No manufactured scarcity, no manufactured volume.** No infinite deck. No variable-reward mechanics. And no fixed daily quota that forces filler on days when nobody clears your bar. Introductions happen when someone genuinely matches — not on a schedule. Slowness and depth are features, not failures.

6. **The whole person, not just a face.** Profiles reveal how someone *lives* — their music, their spaces, what they're reading, where they feel at home. Photos are part of the picture, never the whole picture, never the first thing you judge.

7. **Accountability over disposability.** We design against ghosting and toward kind closure. No juggling dozens of half-alive threads. When something ends, it ends with dignity.

8. **Safety is a product, not a settings page.** Every person is verified as real. Abusive behavior is met with zero tolerance. We will never ask someone — especially women — to "adjust their settings" to feel safe.

9. **Open source as trust.** The matching logic is not a black box. Anyone can read how they are being matched. In this category, that is radical, and it is our credibility.

### When principles conflict

The most important conflict to name out loud: **safety beats privacy.** When protecting people on the platform requires collecting or acting on information we'd otherwise rather not touch, safety wins. We won't pretend we can have absolute privacy *and* absolute safety. We chose: safety first.

The second conflict: **unequal match frequency is a consequence of refusing to fake matches.** A quality threshold means some people receive introductions weekly and some go six weeks without one. That's pairwise compatibility scarcity, not a desirability ranking — but it *feels* indistinguishable. We accept this, we measure it, and we show it to you rather than hiding it. We will never pad a drought with filler matches to make the distribution look equal.

## What AI does here

AI is not decoration and it's not a chatbot. It has three purposes:

**Quality over quantity.** You will never get a deck of options. You get introductions only when someone genuinely clears your bar — derived from your own stated values, dealbreakers, and rankings. AI replaces shallow filtering with curation that refuses to surface filler.

**Genuine compatibility.** The matching engine understands what people *mean* — real alignment on values, communication style, and what they want from life. Every match comes with a human-readable explanation.

**Honest self-knowledge.** When you move on from someone, the system asks why — and learns from your answer. Your preferences evolve based on what you *do*, not just what you say. The AI is your mirror, not your enabler.

**The line:** AI serves depth, safety, and self-knowledge. Never engagement. The moment a feature's real goal is "more time in app," it has become the enemy.
